The little details.

What’s in this flash-sale edit?

120 distinct design listings: 20 photo references + 12 AI-generated fashion images + 88 garment illustrations, inspired by the moodboard. Colour requests live inside each style instead of appearing as duplicate cards. Every listed price is between KSh 500 and KSh 1,000. Each listing has its own image. AI-generated fashion images and illustrations show proposed cuts and details, not existing inventory. Each generated image is labelled on its card and in its details. Photo references do not show every requested colour. Brand marks in reference photos are not a claim of brand affiliation or authenticity.

How do I enquire about a look?

Choose your size, add looks to your bag, then download or copy your enquiry. Share it with EPIC WEAR using the contact details on the main site. This is an inspiration catalogue, not a completed checkout or reservation.

What about fit, delivery and payment?

Sizes XS–XXL are request options, not a guarantee of availability. Ask the studio to confirm measurements, colours, stock, final price, delivery charges and payment arrangements before purchasing. No delivery fee is included in your bag subtotal.
